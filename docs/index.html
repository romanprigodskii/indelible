Self-study · Paper 1 · v0.1

Learning that survives the cold test.

indelible is an open-source skill for Claude Code. It runs your self-study the way a good examiner would: it teaches from sheets you read and then close, marks your work from photos, and brings everything back cold two days later.

Your 2-day recheck is ready. Question 3

Read these instructions first

  1. Read the theory, then close it. Answers written with the explanation in view are practice, not proof.
  2. Practise one question type at a time. You will be tested on all of them, mixed, with no headings.
  3. Nothing counts until it survives 48 hours. Every topic comes back cold two days after you learn it.
  4. Write a check beside every answer, working backwards. Solving it again in your head repeats the same slip.
  5. End each sheet with one line: Least sure of: ___
  6. Every mistake returns after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week and 3 weeks, until it stays.

Materials: Claude Code, Python 3.9 or later. To begin, type:

$ claude plugin marketplace add romanprigodskii/indelible
$ claude plugin install indelible@indelible

Then open Claude Code in an empty folder and say “set me up for…”, naming what you are learning.

Why does indelible make you close the theory before the drills?

Mark scheme

B1 With the explanation in view, every answer is a lookup. It feels like knowing, and it isn’t.

B1 So theory comes on its own sheet. You read it, close it, and only then get the drills. Reopening is allowed, but it is written down, and those answers count as practice.

Theory sheet

The middle number

Sort the list, then take the value in the middle: 3, 9, 4, 12, 7 sorts to 3, 4, 7, 9, 12.

Add 100 to that list and the average jumps from 7 to 22.5. The middle number only moves to 8.

Even count: take the average of the two middle values.

Close this sheet now. The drills come separately.

Drills come in blocks of one kind. Tests are mixed, with no headings. Why both?

Mark scheme

B1 Six in a row of one kind makes the step automatic.

B1 A real exam never says which topic a question is from, so anything that measures you is shuffled and unlabelled. You have to recognise the problem before you can solve it.

      Where it works

      Claude CodeSupported. Terminal, desktop app and IDE extensions.
      CoworkShould work with a shared folder. Untested in v0.1.
      claude.ai chat, mobileA limited manual mode for now: files don’t persist between chats there.

      Your data

      • Plain files in a folder you choose, on your own computer.
      • The scripts make no network requests. Nothing is sent to the author.
      • Calendar entries only through your own connector, after a preview and your yes.
      • Answer keys stay sealed until your attempt is filed.

      Privacy policy · What it runs, writes and sends

      Honest limits

      A skill cannot physically stop the model from breaking its own rules, so the rules that matter most do not rely on instructions alone. The only route to a printable sheet runs a sheet checker. The only route to an answer key needs your attempt filed first. The next session finds anything the last one skipped, and does it first.

      The method was developed with one learner over about four weeks. That is not a controlled study. The principles come from well-studied effects: retrieval practice (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006), spacing (Cepeda et al., 2006) and interleaving (Rohrer & Taylor, 2007). The numbers, like 48 hours and six questions a block, are defaults you can change.
