# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who teach themselves with Claude and have a deadline or a goal: an exam, a course final, an interview, a language, a programming skill. Most use Claude Code; many are developers or university students, some are professionals picking up a new field. They arrive from GitHub, Anthropic's plugin directory, or a post on Reddit or Hacker News, usually on a laptop, and decide within a minute whether to install. Many have already tried "study with AI" chats that felt productive in the moment and didn't stick a week later. Some read English as a second language.

## Product Purpose

indelible is an open-source Claude skill that runs self-study the way a strict, organised tutor would: a short onboarding interview, a diagnostic before any teaching, theory on a sheet that is read and then closed, drills on paper with a written backwards check beside every answer, a cold recheck two days later, spaced review of every mistake, and a plan that lands in the learner's calendar with their consent.

The site exists to make the method understood in seconds, to show it working rather than claim it, and to get the install. Success: after thirty seconds a visitor can say what a 2-day recheck is and why same-day scores don't count, and they copy the install command.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, warm, encouraging. A demanding tutor who believes in you: exact about mistakes, never flattering, never cold. The voice is plain and confident, in short sentences. It states evidence with its label ("measured", "practice", "one learner, not a controlled study") and admits its limits openly.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS: gradients, glowing heroes, big fake metrics, "supercharge your learning".
- Gamified edtech: mascots, streak flames, confetti, XP bars, badges.
- Stiff university sites: crests, stock photos of graduates, dense walls of text.
- Productivity-guru hype: "10x your brain", countdown timers, bold testimonials.

## Design Principles

1. **Practise what it preaches.** Every claim about the method is shown on the page: a sheet you read and then close, a check line catching a slip, a recheck two days later. No claim without a demonstration.
2. **Paper and ink, not pixels and glow.** Physical study objects carry the identity: the answer sheet, handwriting, the marker's pen, the ink check.
3. **Honest numbers only.** No invented statistics, testimonials or user counts. Every number carries its label.
4. **Warm rigour.** Firm about the method, kind to the reader. Mistakes are information, not failure.
5. **One idea per fold.** Each section teaches one rule and nothing else.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Plain words and short sentences for second-language readers. Every animation has a reduced-motion alternative, and every demo works from the keyboard. No information is carried by colour alone: marker's-pen marks are paired with symbols (a tick, a cross, a circled number).
